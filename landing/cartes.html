How it works

Every roll is a surprise. Every card has a value, a rarity and combat potential.

01

Rolls & rarity

/roll pulls a random card every hour (per-server timer). Common, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, plus rare animated Secret cards.

19,000+ characters
02

Essence economy

Every roll earns Essences. Recycle your duplicates, buy from the shop, fund your guild and unlock cosmetics.

03

Fusion & stars

Fuse your duplicates to add stars (up to 5) and climb the rarity ladder with /cardup.

04

Cosmetic borders

Dress your best cards in gold, frost, void, hell and leaf borders bought from the shop.

05

Trades & wishlist

Trade several cards at once, even uneven deals, add cards to your wishlist and get notified when they drop.

06

Live raid bosses

Up to 5 players take on a boss as a team (DPS, Tank, Healer), tracked live on the web, with an A-to-F grade per player that decides the loot.
